🟢 Level 2 — Query Operators

Now we'll test whether you actually understand MongoDB's query operators.

8. Products under ₹1,000

Find products where:

price < 1000
db.products.find({price:{$lt:1000}})
---------------------------------------
9. Products between ₹500 and ₹2,000

Use:

$gte
$lte
db.products.find({price:{$gt:500,$lt:2000}})
-----------------------------------------
10. Products with stock greater than 20

Use:

$gt
db.products.find({stock:{$gt:20}})
---------------------------------
11. Products with zero stock

Find products where:

stock = 0
 db.products.find({stock:0})
12. Electronics products

Find products where:

category = "electronics"
db.products.find({category:'electronics'})
--------------------------------------
13. Multiple conditions

Find products where:

category = "electronics"
AND
price < 2000
 db.products.find({$and:[{category:'electronics'},{price:{$lt:2000}}]})
----------------------------------
14. OR condition

Find products where:

category = "electronics"
OR
category = "clothing"

Use:

$or
db.products.find({$or:[{category:'electronics'},{category:'clothing'}]})
15. Products with good ratings

Find products where:

rating >= 4
db.products.find({rating:{$gte:4}})
