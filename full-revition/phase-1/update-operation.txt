7. updateOne and updateMany
Exercises 33–41
33. Update the price of Laptop to ₹55,000.
db.products.updateMany({name:'Laptop'},{$set:{price:55000}})
----------------------------------------------
34. Change the stock of Mobile to 15.
db.products.updateMany({name:'Mobile'},{$set:{stock:15}})
-----------------------------------------
35. Update the brand of SQL Book to Pearson.
db.products.updateMany({brand:'SQL Book'},{$set:{brand:'Pearson'}})
--------------------------------------------------
36. Increase the price of every product in the Electronics category by 10%.
 db.products.updateMany({category:'Electronics'},{$mul:{price:1.10}})
------------------------------------------------
37. Reduce the stock of all products by 2.
db.products.updateMany({},{$inc:{stock:-2}})
-----------------------------------------------
38. Change isAvailable to false for all products with stock equal to zero.
 db.products.updateMany({stock:0},{$set:{isAvailable:false}})
----------------------------------------------
39. Add a new field called discount to every product, with a value of 5.
db.products.updateMany({},{$set:{discount:5}})
-------------------------------------------------------
40. Update a product's rating and stock in a single operation.
db.products.updateMany({},{$set:{rating:4.8},$inc:{stock:10}})
----------------------------------------------------------
41. Use $set, $inc, and $unset to modify product documents. Explain the difference between these operators.
db.products.updateOne({name:'Wireless Mouse'},{$set:{price:2000}})
 db.products.updateOne({name:'Wireless Mouse'},{$inc:{stock:10}})
 db.products.updateOne({name:'Wireless Mouse'},{$unset:{discount:""}})
