23. Retrieve all products.

---------------------------------------------
27. Find products with stock less than or equal to 10.
db.products.find({stock:{$lte:10}})
-----------------------------------------------
28. Find products with a rating greater than or equal to 4.5.
db.products.find({rating:{$gte:4.5}})
---------------------------------------------
29. Find a product by its name using findOne().
db.products.findOne({name:"Laptop"})
---------------------------------------------
30. Find all products where isAvailable is false.
db.products.find({isAvailable:false})
------------------------------------------
