8. deleteOne and deleteMany
Exercises 42–47
42. Delete the product named Keyboard.
 db.products.deleteMany({name:'Keyboard'})
 ----------------------------------------------
43. Delete a customer using their email address.
db.customers.deleteMany({email:{$exists:true,$ne:null}})
-------------------------------------------------
44. Delete all products belonging to the Clothing category.
 db.products.deleteMany({category:'Clothing'})
 -----------------------------------------
45. Delete all products whose stock is zero.
 db.products.deleteMany({stock:0})
 ---------------------------------------
46. Delete all products with a rating below 3.
db.products.deleteMany({rating:{$lt:3}})
-------------------------------------------
47. Delete all documents from a temporary collection without dropping the collection itself.
db.products.deleteMany({})
