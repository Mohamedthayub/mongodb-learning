Use your existing products collection.
1. Find products with a price greater than ₹1,000 and stock greater than 5.
db.products.find({$and:[{price:{$gt:1000}},{stock:{$gt:5}}]})
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Find products priced below ₹1,000 or above ₹20,000.
db.products.find({$or:[{price:{$lt:1000}},{price:{$gt:20000}}]})
----------------------------------------------------
3. Find products belonging to Electronics or Books.
db.products.find({$or:[{category:'Electronics'},{category:'Books'}]})
---------------------------------------------------------------
4. Find products whose brand is not Samsung.
db.products.find({brand:{$ne:'Sumsung'}})
------------------------------------------------
5. Find products whose stock is between 5 and 20.
db.products.find({stock:{$gt:5,$lt:20}})
-------------------------------------------
6. Find products whose name starts with the letter P.
db.products.find({name:{$regex:/^P/}})
--------------------------------------------------
7. Find products whose name contains Book, case-insensitively.
db.products.find({name:{$regex:"Book",$options:'i'}})
-------------------------------------------------------
8. Find products that have a discount field.
db.products.find({discount:{$exists:true}})
---------------------------------------------------
9. Find products where the rating field is a number.
db.products.find({rating:{type:"number"}})
---------------------------------------------
10. Find products that are either unavailable or have stock equal to zero
db.products.find({$or:[{available:false},{stock:0}]})
---------------------------------------------------------
